What is the definition of a modern hypercar

The definition of a modern hypercar is a car that is closer to a racing car but remain what we call "street legal". One good example of a modern hypercar is the Koenigsegg Jesko which is fully street legal globally. Just to mention another great example the Bugatti Veyron is also a fully street legal hypercar.
